> - A `Proposed` ADR can be edited freely while it is discussed.
> - Once `Accepted`, the ADR is **immutable**. If the decision changes, 
>   write a new ADR and change only this one's status to 
>   `Superseded by ADR-0YY`.
> - Write an ADR when a decision has meaningful trade-offs (NFR-7): 
>   if a reasonable developer could have chosen differently, record why 
>   you didn't.

current state, without arguing for an option yet.

## Decision drivers

What matters most when choosing (e.g. learning value, simplicity, 
consistency with existing code, cost, market relevance, testability).

## Decision

*Accepted*: the chosen option and **why**, referring to the drivers. 
*Proposed*: write **Recommendation** instead, with its reasoning; the 
developer makes the final call.

## Consequences

- **Positive**: what becomes easier or better.
- **Negative**: what it costs, what becomes harder, what we accept.
- **Follow-ups**: docs to update, debt created or resolved, when to 
  revisit.
